What to produce (the JSON schema is enforced; fill every field):
- has_speech: true if the video has spoken words (voice-over or on-camera speech); false if it is music/sound-only with on-screen text.
- idea.topic: the broad topic in 3-6 words. idea.idea_seed: 1-2 sentences on the core idea and why it carries the video. idea.unique_angle: 1-2 sentences on what makes this take different, contrarian or first-mover.
- hook: 3-4 beats covering roughly the first 3 seconds. "time" is a second offset like "0.0s" or "1.4s". "line" is the exact spoken line, or the on-screen text / visual beat if nothing is said. "note" is why that beat retains, 1-2 sentences.
- structure.summary: e.g. "5 beats over 38s". structure.beats: 3-6 beats in chronological order covering the whole runtime. "label" is one UPPERCASE word (HOOK, FLIP, ESCALATION, RECEIPTS, PAYOFF...). "start"/"end" are m:ss (e.g. "0:03"), never raw seconds. "pct" is the integer share of runtime; all pct values must sum to 100. "highlight" is "red" for the hook beat, "volt" for the payoff/loop beat, null otherwise.
- visual: 3-5 cards describing what you actually SEE: framing (talking head, b-roll, screen recording, animation), caption style and placement, cut rate, zooms, text overlays, colour and lighting, and roughly what share of runtime each takes. Be specific; only state what is visible in the frames.
- transcript: the full transcript grouped into readable passages of 1-3 sentences (about one passage per 10-15 seconds), in order, covering the whole video, each with its m:ss start time. Label the notable passages ("hook", "reframe", "receipts", "loop point"); null for the rest. If has_speech is false, transcribe the on-screen text in order instead.

Rules:
- Ground every claim in what is said or shown. Do not invent specifics you cannot see or hear.
- Ignore platform chrome: watermarks, usernames, like/comment counters.
- Transcribe in the spoken language; write the analysis in English.
- Keep notes tight and tactical, written for a creator deciding what to copy.
