# About ViewsMax

ViewsMax (https://viewsmax.com) is a social posting + sales tracking SaaS for
creators and marketers: make content that makes sales.

## What it does

- **Post everywhere from one place.** Compose once, publish or schedule to
  YouTube, TikTok, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, Threads, Instagram, and Bluesky.
  Drafts, a content calendar, and automatic retries per platform.
- **Track what sells.** Create an *offer* (a product or campaign you promote),
  generate *tracked links* for it, and place them in videos, posts, emails, or
  bios. ViewsMax records clicks, conversions, and revenue per link.
- **Attribution, not vanity metrics.** See which platform, which post, and
  which time of day actually drives sales — funnels, customer journeys,
  first-touch vs last-touch comparison, time-to-convert, and a
  revenue-per-view leaderboard.
- **AI-agent ready.** An MCP server and REST API let AI assistants (Claude,
  ChatGPT, Cursor, OpenClaw, Hermes, …) post and read analytics on a user's
  behalf. See [Connect your AI](https://viewsmax.com/ai.md).

## Core concepts

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Post | One piece of content fanned out to one or more platforms; each platform target reports its own publish status. |
| Offer | A product/campaign being promoted, with an offer URL and conversion value. |
| Tracked link | A short link tied to an offer; records clicks and attributes conversions. |
| Conversion | A sale or goal completion attributed back to the link/post that drove it. |

## Who it's for

Creators, solo marketers, and agencies who publish on several platforms and
want to know which content generates revenue — not just views.
