# ViewsMax Pricing

Free to start (no card required). Paid plans are monthly, in USD.
Sign up at https://viewsmax.com/auth — plan checkout at https://viewsmax.com/checkout.

| Plan | Price | Channels | Offers | Posts / month | AI credits / month |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | — | — | — (content review & topic tools) | 100 one-time welcome credits |
| Starter | $29/mo | 5 | 1 | 400 | 1,000 |
| Creator (most popular) | $39/mo | 30 | 5 | Unlimited | 2,500 |
| Pro | $69/mo | Unlimited | 10 | Unlimited | 5,000 |
| Agency | $109/mo | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | 10,000 |

AI credits pay for AI-agent (MCP) tool calls: each tool call spends credits
(read-only tools 1, actions 5, heavy research tools such as AI breakdowns up
to 25 — every tool states its cost). Credits reset with each billing cycle;
a call is refused when the balance is too low, and failed calls are free.

- **Starter** — "Launch your first offer." For creators just getting started.
- **Creator** — "For brands that sell." Growing creators running multiple offers.
- **Pro** — "Scale every channel." Professionals scaling their reach.
- **Agency** — "For teams & agencies." Unlimited everything.

Every paid plan includes:

- Post and schedule to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Threads and Bluesky
- Multiple accounts per platform, grouped into brands
- Tracked links with click, conversion and revenue attribution
- Outlier research on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram, with AI breakdowns
- Boosts: auto-repost and auto-promo when a post takes off (X)
- MCP server and API keys for Claude, ChatGPT and other agents (see https://viewsmax.com/ai.md)
- Comment chains, X threads and custom thumbnails
- Beehiiv newsletter reach tracking
- Human support

The offer limit is enforced per account; channel and post limits are shown on
each plan. Current values are always available from the authenticated API
(`GET /api/plans`).
