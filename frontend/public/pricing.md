# ViewsMax Pricing

Free to start (no card required). Paid plans are monthly, in USD.
Sign up at https://viewsmax.com/auth — plan checkout at https://viewsmax.com/checkout.

| Plan | Price | Channels | Offers | Posts / month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | — | — | — (content review & topic tools) |
| Starter | $29/mo | 5 | 1 | 400 |
| Creator (most popular) | $59/mo | 30 | 5 | Unlimited |
| Pro | $99/mo | Unlimited | 10 | Unlimited |
| Agency | $149/mo | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |

- **Starter** — "Launch your first offer." For creators just getting started.
- **Creator** — "For brands that sell." Growing creators running multiple offers.
- **Pro** — "Scale every channel." Professionals scaling their reach.
- **Agency** — "For teams & agencies." Unlimited everything.

All paid plans include cross-platform posting/scheduling (YouTube, TikTok, X,
LinkedIn, Threads, Instagram, Bluesky), tracked links with click/conversion/
revenue attribution, analytics dashboards, and AI agent access (MCP + API
key — see https://viewsmax.com/ai.md).

Limits are enforced per account; current values are always available from the
authenticated API (`GET /api/plans`).
